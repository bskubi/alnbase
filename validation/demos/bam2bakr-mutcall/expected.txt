read length 60, T at offsets [3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35, 39, 43, 47, 51, 55, 59]
qual.bam: T->C at offset 11 (Phred 10) and offset 15 (Phred 5)
ends.bam: all 15 T's mutated; 3 of them at offsets [3, 55, 59], inside the --minDist 5 end zones
=== 1. What is --minQual measured in? ===
One T->C at Phred 10, one at Phred 5, both far from the read ends.

--minQual    TC     nT     implied Phred cutoff
30           2      15     q > -3
33           2      15     q > 0
34           2      15     q > 1
40           1      14     q > 7
44           0      13     q > 11
76           0      0      q > 43

The comparison is 'phred + 33 > minQual', so the shipped value 40 is a
Phred cutoff of 8, and any value of 33 or less disables the filter.

=== 2. Numerator and denominator do not share filters ===
Every T in the read is mutated, so the true per-T rate is 1.000.
nT (trials) = 15   TC (mutations) = 12   reported rate = 0.800

nT counts every T that passes the base-quality filter. TC additionally
requires the T to be more than --minDist bases from either end of the
aligned block, so the T's in the two end zones are trials that can never
become mutations. The rate is biased low by about 2*minDist/readlength.
